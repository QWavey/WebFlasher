Flash

Step 01 Plug in device (DFU mode)

Hold the BOOT button on the board while plugging the device into USB. Release after one second. The device is now waiting for firmware and will not appear as a keyboard.

On Windows, use Zadig once per PC to bind the DFU device to the WinUSB driver, or the browser will not see it.

Step 02 Connect

Pick the device in the browser prompt. Look for VID 03EB, PID 2FF6.

Step 03 Flash

Uses the bundled firmware.hex unless you pick your own. Erase, program, and launch run in sequence.
